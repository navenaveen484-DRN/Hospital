HospiCare Regulatory & Compliance Center

Legal Agreements, HIPAA Safeguards & Clinical Safety

1. Protected Health Information (PHI) & Privacy

HospiCare adheres strictly to the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) Privacy Rule (45 CFR Part 160 and Part 164, Subparts A and E). All medical records, diagnostic observations, appointment schedules, and patient demographic indicators processed through this system are designated as electronic Protected Health Information (ePHI).

2. Data Collection and Permitted Disclosures

We do not sell, license, or monetize clinical data. Data collected is strictly utilized for:

  • Facilitating inpatient and outpatient clinical workflows and medical history evaluation.
  • Processing electronic insurance claims (EDI 270/271 Eligibility, EDI 837P Claims, EDI 278 Prior Authorization).
  • Autonomous administrative optimization with human-in-the-loop validation.

3. Patient Rights and Access Logs

Patients possess the statutory right under HIPAA to request an accounting of disclosures. HospiCare maintains an immutable cryptographic audit ledger recording every query, modification, export, and viewing event associated with each medical record identifier.

1. Authorized Clinical Users Only

HospiCare is an enterprise healthcare management application licensed exclusively to verified medical facilities, attending physicians, nursing staff, clinical administrators, and authenticated healthcare personnel. Unauthorized access attempts violate 18 U.S.C. § 1030 (Computer Fraud and Abuse Act) and will be prosecuted.

2. Clinical Disclaimers & Emergency Protocols

This software functions as a clinical administrative orchestration tool and clinical decision support (CDS) facilitator. It is not an automated medical emergency triage dispatch mechanism. In life-threatening emergencies, personnel must activate immediate hospital emergency code systems (Code Blue / Code Red) directly.

3. Credential Confidentiality

Staff members must never share access keys, passwords, or multi-factor biometric tokens. Sessions automatically time out after inactivity to safeguard unattended clinical workstations.

1. HIPAA Security Rule Technical Safeguards (45 CFR § 164.312)

  • Access Control (§ 164.312(a)(1)): Unique user identification, emergency “break-glass” access procedures, automatic session logoff, and encrypted session tokens.
  • Audit Controls (§ 164.312(b)): Comprehensive tamper-evident logging of all ePHI access, modifications, exports, and administrative overrides.
  • Integrity Controls (§ 164.312(c)(1)): Checksums and signature verification to protect ePHI from improper alteration or destruction.
  • Transmission Security (§ 164.312(e)(1)): Mandatory TLS 1.3 encryption in transit for all client-server exchanges and EDI transmission endpoints.

2. Business Associate Agreement (BAA)

HospiCare operates under formal Business Associate Agreements with our hosting infrastructure providers and healthcare network partners, ensuring end-to-end contractual compliance across all computing, storage, and networking layers.

1. Autonomous AI Agent Architectural Guardrails

The HospiCare Autonomous AI Administrative Agent executes actions within a structured 5-Phase Deterministic State Machine (Reasoning → Query → Act → Verify → Resolve). The agent operates under strict medical guardrails aligned with AMA Clinical AI recommendations:

  • Autonomous Medication Blocking: The agent is programmatically prohibited from autonomously changing dosages, writing controlled substance prescriptions, or overriding clinical allergy flags without manual physician co-signature.
  • Confidence Gating: All autonomous actions require an algorithmic confidence threshold ≥ 85%. Proposals below threshold trigger mandatory human clinical review.
  • Complete Audit Trail: Every intermediate reasoning step, retrieved database record, tool invocation, and verification metric is permanently stamped into the clinical event log.

1. Enterprise High-Availability Service Level Agreement

HospiCare Enterprise provides a guaranteed 99.99% system availability SLA for emergency department and inpatient core management functions. Automated multi-region database failover and sub-second transaction replication ensure business continuity in catastrophic disaster scenarios.

2. Scheduled Maintenance Windows

Routine system upgrades and schema migrations occur exclusively during designated low-census maintenance windows with zero-downtime rolling deployments.
